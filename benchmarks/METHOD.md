# What is being measured

These are independent baseline experiments implemented with existing packages, not unchanged reproductions of a published paper. The geometry, meshes, and thresholds are benchmark defaults, not requirements for a future solver.

## The two problems

**Regular capacitor:** a 100 nm wide, 100 nm tall cross-section, with silicon in the bottom 90 nm and oxide in the top 10 nm. Coordinates are normalized to a unit square. Relative permittivities are 11.7 and 3.9. Solve `-div(epsilon_r grad(u)) = 0`, with 0 V at the bottom, 1 V at the top, insulated sides, and continuous potential and normal displacement at the interface. Silicon is a charge-free dielectric here; there are no carriers or doping.

The exact answer is `u = R(y) / R(1)`, where `R(y) = min(y, 0.9)/11.7 + max(y-0.9, 0)/3.9`. The interface is at 0.75 V. The answer is constant horizontally and linear within each layer, so aligned linear elements can represent it exactly. Increasing the mesh makes the calculation larger but does not introduce new physics.

**Sine:** solve `-laplacian(u) = 2*pi^2*sin(pi*x)*sin(pi*y)` on the unit square, with zero voltage on all four edges. The exact answer is `u = sin(pi*x)*sin(pi*y)`. Its curvature gives a meaningful refinement test.

## Tools and meshes

Both tools use the same triangular geometry generated by scikit-fem, but independently assemble and solve their equations. At subdivision count N there are `(N+1)^2` unique vertices and `2*N^2` triangles. The regular case assigns `3*N/4` vertical rows to silicon and `N/4` to oxide, exactly aligning the interface. Sine uses uniform spacing. Free physical potential counts are `N^2-1` for regular and `(N-1)^2` for sine; DEVSIM has additional contact/interface equations internally.

- **DEVSIM 2.11.0:** edge flux formulation, MKL PARDISO direct solves inside its DC Newton iteration, absolute and relative update tolerances `1e-10`, maximum 20 iterations. Each repetition starts from zero potential.
- **scikit-fem 12.0.2 / SciPy 1.18.1:** triangular P1 elements, SuperLU factorization with COLAMD ordering. Material-form quadrature is order 2; sine RHS quadrature is order 6.
- Arrays and exports are float64. Each measured repetition constructs a new mesh, equations, and factorization. No factorization is reused across repetitions.
- One worker runs at a time, with one computation thread requested and loaded thread pools checked. No CPU affinity or power-policy changes are made; background activity is uncontrolled.

## Timing and correctness

`pipeline_s` is one wall-clock timer from mesh construction through a completed file write: mesh, assembly, factorization, solving, interpolation, and export. It excludes process startup, package imports, accuracy checks, teardown, and result-log serialization. The report shows median and interquartile range across five measured repetitions; `repeat=-1` is the excluded warm-up.

Component columns are also saved. `solver_setup_s` measures explicit SuperLU factorization and is blank for DEVSIM. DEVSIM's `solve_s` includes its internal assembly and factorization, so comparing `solve_s` alone is not an equal-scope comparison. Do not add the total to its component timers. Memory in `worker.json` covers the whole worker lifetime, including imports, warm-up, interpolation, and checking.

All tools export an uncompressed 512 by 512 NumPy array at uniform cell centers, indexed `[y, x]`, using piecewise-linear interpolation on the original triangles. Writes are closed but not forced to durable disk storage. A fixed export grid keeps output cost comparable.

Every repetition checks finite values, boundary error below `1e-10`, and relative grid error below `1e-10` for regular or `0.02` for sine. The final repetition also integrates squared error over native triangles, using quadrature orders 6 and 8. Sine integrals must agree within 1%; its convergence order must be between 1.8 and 2.2. Regular integrated error must stay below `1e-10`. The FEM algebraic residual and DEVSIM convergence are checked separately. These calculations occur outside the timer.

Failures stop the run and leave the job settings, exit status, and traceback log in its run folder. The included full run completed all 60 measured solves and 12 warm-ups successfully. Its original audit also checked all 72 exported arrays; those arrays remain local and are regenerated by rerunning.

## Extending the benchmark

Implement the same adapter operations (`solve`, `sample`, `stats`, `close`) and expose coordinates, triangles, and nodal voltage for the common checks. Use the analytical formulas as truth. Keep problem definitions fixed; give changed physics a separate case. These two tests can check a future surrogate's output but cannot establish generalization across devices or replace separate training and validation datasets.

Package references: [DEVSIM](https://github.com/devsim/devsim), [scikit-fem](https://github.com/kinnala/scikit-fem), [SciPy](https://github.com/scipy/scipy). FEniCSx and COMSOL were not run.
